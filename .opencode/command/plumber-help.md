Show the plumber quick reference. One shot, change nothing. Levels: /plumber lite (writes the fix, flags the cleaner redesign in one line), /plumber (full, default: root cause then design check then remove then simplest), /plumber ultra (won't write a line until root cause is identified). Commands: /plumber-diagnose (explicit diagnosis before acting), /plumber-review (patchwork review of current changes), /plumber-audit (whole-repo patchwork audit), /plumber-help (this card). Deactivate with 'stop plumber', 'normal mode', or /plumber off.
